Catholic life

Life in Christ is based on living a prayerful life and adhearing to the commandments mainly
the two main commandments by Jesus :

  1. love the lord your God with all your strength and with all your might.
  2. love your neighbour as you love yourself.
You cannot say that you love Christ if you do not love your neighbour.Remember the golden rule do to the other what you would like to be done to you.

prayerful life

Christian life is strengthened by prayers which is a conversation with God our creator.
The rosary and other chaplets are few guides on the prayer life of a christian,pray for Gods holy spirit in you life to guide you ,
strengthen you and to enable you to understand the holy scriptures.Ensure that you read the holy scriptures not to gain knowledge to challenge
as hypocreates do but to understan Gods will in your life.

Intiation sacraments
baptism

sacraments of healing
sacrament of reconciliation

sacraments of service
